## 8. Purpose of the processing of personal data

The purpose is to examine how members of the public experience and evaluate AI chatbots that discuss Finnish politics: communication style, use of language, framing, means of persuasion, and whether people notice bias or attempts to influence them. Conversations and survey answers are analysed qualitatively and quantitatively for (a) scientific publications and presentations, (b) teaching, and (c) improving the chatbot and the study design.

The chatbot may be instructed to express a particular political viewpoint. Whether this applies to a given conversation, and which viewpoint, is not disclosed before the conversation, because the study asks whether people notice it. It is explained on the final page after the survey.

Personal data are processed only for scientific research. They are not used for decisions concerning you, marketing, profiling, or training AI models.

## 10. Personal data included in the research data

Collected by the Vaalikeskustelu application and stored on the CSC Rahti platform in Finland:
- a random session identifier (UUID);
- timestamps (start, confirmation of participation, each message, survey submission);
- your language choice; your confirmation that you are at least 18 (yes);
- the experimental condition assigned to your session (the party the chatbot was instructed to represent and the framing variant) and the starter topic you chose;
- every message you type and every chatbot reply (message text encrypted at rest);
- technical metadata of the language-model calls (token counts [and model identifier, if stored]);
- your survey answers (chatbot usage, which chatbots you use, prior political chats, attitude to chatbots on a 1 to 5 scale, free reflection, ratings of speed, topic variety, detected bias, terminology and persuasion on a 1 to 5 scale, free-text observations).

Not collected by the application: name, email, telephone, IP address, device identifiers, location, account. No cookies; the browser's local storage holds only your language choice and the event access code.

Platform logs: the CSC Rahti routing layer may record your device's IP address in short-lived technical logs [verify retention with CSC]. The researchers do not use these logs and cannot link them to research data.

The research data are pseudonymous: the researchers cannot identify you from them unless you give us your session code (section 17) or you have typed identifying information. Identifying details volunteered in free text are removed in a documented screening step before analysis.
